GET GOOD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Get Good does not collect personal information, does not require an account, and does not sell your data. Ever.

Who we are

Get Good is an iOS app developed and operated by Low Signal Labs (Andrew Dobrow), based in Hobe Sound, Florida. You can reach us at anjrued123@gmail.com.

What data Get Good collects

Get Good collects as little data as possible.

  • Skill input: When you type a skill and tap to generate a plan, that text and your selected experience level are sent to our server to generate your plan using AI. We do not store this input after your plan is generated.
  • Your plans: The plans and progress you create are stored locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to us.
  • Subscription status: If you subscribe, your purchase is processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat. We receive only your subscription status (active or not) — no payment details, no billing information.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any contact information
  • We do not require account creation
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking pixels
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts

AI processing

When you generate a plan, your skill input and experience level are sent to our backend server (hosted on Vercel) which uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate your plan. Anthropic processes this input to generate a response and does not store it for model training by default. Our server does not log or retain your input after the plan is returned to your device.

You can review Anthropic's privacy practices at anthropic.com/privacy.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous device identifier and your subscription state. No payment card information is ever shared with us or RevenueCat. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children's privacy

Get Good is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through our app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data retention and deletion

Because all your plan data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting individual skills within the app or by uninstalling Get Good. We have no server-side copy of your plans to delete, because we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Get Good after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
